Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is Glow?

Glow is a Windows app that lights the edges of your screen in time with your music. It follows your system audio and the beat, can take colors from your album art, and has an optional full-screen now-playing view with your cover, synced lyrics and a clock.

Can I choose my own color?

Yes. Choose Album art (colors come from the current cover), Rainbow, or Custom with two colors of your own.

Can I adjust how it reacts?

Yes. Sliders control brightness, size, reaction, sensitivity and speed. There's also a beat delay for Bluetooth headphones, and a frame rate setting that follows your monitor (up to 144 Hz) by default.

What is the now-playing screen?

A full-screen view with your album cover, synced lyrics and a clock, wrapped in the glow. Set it to appear when your PC is idle (after 30 seconds by default, only while music plays) or right away. Open or close it any time with Ctrl+Alt+N or from the tray. Esc or a click closes it.

Which music apps work?

The light works with anything your PC plays. Cover colors, the now-playing screen and lyrics use the track info your player shares with Windows, which most players and browsers do. If yours doesn't, the light still works.

Does it work with Bluetooth headphones?

Yes. Bluetooth audio arrives late, so raise Beat delay (up to 500 ms) until the glow lands on the beat you hear.

Does Glow record my audio or use the internet?

Audio is analyzed on your PC to drive the light and is never saved or sent anywhere. Lyrics and HD album art are the only online lookups: Glow sends the current song's title and artist to LRCLIB (lyrics), and to Apple's iTunes Search and Deezer (album art). Turn off HD album art in the Display tab, or set the now-playing screen to show only the album cover, to skip lookups.

Does it work on more than one monitor?

Glow currently lights your primary monitor.

What do I need to run Glow?

A PC running Windows 10 or Windows 11 and an audio output device. A GPU helps the now-playing screen run smoothly, and Glow can prefer your NVIDIA GPU if you have one.
